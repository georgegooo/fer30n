# Price Action Reversal Checklist / قائمة تحقق الانعكاس (Price Action)

Use this checklist to structure (not guarantee) a price-action-based reversal entry.

1. **Level quality** — Is the support/resistance level well-tested (multiple prior touches with strong reactions)? *(هل المستوى مُختبر جيدًا بارتدادات قوية سابقة؟)*
2. **Rejection pattern** — Is there a recognizable rejection candle (pin bar, engulfing, doji) at the level? *(هل توجد شمعة رفض واضحة عند المستوى؟)*
3. **Multi-candle confirmation** — Does the candle after the rejection confirm direction (e.g., a strong close in the new direction) rather than immediately reversing back? *(هل تؤكد الشمعة التالية الاتجاه؟)*
4. **Higher-timeframe context** — Does this reversal align with (or at least not strongly fight) the higher-timeframe trend? *(هل يتوافق الانعكاس مع اتجاه الفريم الأكبر أو على الأقل لا يعاكسه بقوة؟)*
5. **Volume/participation (if available)** — Is there any sign of increased participation on the reversal candle rather than a low-volume drift? *(هل توجد زيادة في المشاركة عند شمعة الانعكاس؟)*
6. **Risk definition** — Is the stop placed beyond the rejection candle's wick / pattern extreme? *(هل الوقف خلف أقصى نقطة النموذج؟)*
7. **False-breakout awareness** — If this follows a recent breakout of the same level, has enough time passed to rule out an immediate fakeout back through it? *(إذا كان هناك اختراق حديث، هل تم استبعاد احتمال الاختراق الكاذب؟)*

If items 1, 2, and 6 are not all present, treat the setup as low-confidence.

⚠️ This checklist organizes analysis; it does not predict outcomes or guarantee profit. Educational content only, not financial advice.
