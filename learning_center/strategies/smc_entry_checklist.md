# SMC Entry Checklist / قائمة تحقق الدخول (SMC)

Use this checklist to structure (not guarantee) an SMC-based entry decision.

1. **Higher-timeframe bias** — Is there a clear HH/HL (bullish) or LH/LL (bearish) structure on the higher timeframe? *(اتجاه الفريم الكبير واضح؟)*
2. **Zone of interest** — Is there an unmitigated order block or FVG aligned with that bias, ideally inside a discount zone (longs) or premium zone (shorts)? *(هل توجد منطقة اهتمام غير ملموسة متوافقة مع الاتجاه؟)*
3. **Liquidity sweep** — Has a nearby swing high/low (or equal highs/lows) been swept, ideally just before or as price reaches the zone of interest? *(هل حدث اصطياد سيولة قريب من المنطقة؟)*
4. **Structure confirmation** — Has a CHoCH (and ideally a follow-up BOS) appeared on a lower timeframe after the sweep? *(هل تأكد تغيّر الهيكل على فريم أصغر؟)*
5. **Risk definition** — Is there a clear invalidation point (beyond the sweep's extreme / beyond the order block) to place the stop? *(هل يوجد مستوى إبطال واضح لوضع وقف الخسارة؟)*
6. **Target** — Is there a logical next liquidity pool or opposing order block to target, and does the resulting RRR meet your minimum threshold? *(هل يوجد هدف منطقي بعائد مخاطرة مقبول؟)*
7. **News filter** — Is a high-impact release (NFP/CPI/FOMC) due within the next 30–60 minutes? If so, consider standing aside or reducing size. *(هل هناك خبر مؤثر قريب؟)*
8. **Risk sizing** — Does the position size, given the stop distance, keep risk within your fixed per-trade percentage? *(هل حجم الصفقة يحترم نسبة المخاطرة الثابتة؟)*

If any of items 1, 3, 5, or 8 is missing, the setup quality is materially weaker — treat these four as the non-negotiable minimum.

⚠️ This checklist organizes analysis; it does not predict outcomes or guarantee profit. Educational content only, not financial advice.
