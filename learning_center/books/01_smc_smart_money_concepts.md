# Smart Money Concepts (SMC) — Complete Reference

## ملخص سريع (عربي)
"سمارت موني كونسبتس" هو إطار لقراءة السوق من زاوية كبار اللاعبين (البنوك والمؤسسات) بدلاً من المؤشرات التقليدية. الفكرة الأساسية: السعر يتحرك ليجمع سيولة (أوامر وقف الخسارة والدخول المتراكمة) قبل أن يعكس اتجاهه أو يكمله. أهم أدوات القراءة: **Order Blocks** (آخر شمعة معاكسة قبل حركة قوية)، **Liquidity Sweep** (اختراق قمة/قاع لجمع الأوامر ثم الرجوع)، **BOS/CHoCH** (استمرار أو تغيّر هيكل الاتجاه)، **Fair Value Gap** (فجوة سعرية تُملأ لاحقًا)، و**Premium/Discount Zones** (مناطق الشراء الرخيص والبيع الغالي داخل الرينج).

---

## 1. Market Structure (هيكل السوق)
- **Higher High (HH) / Higher Low (HL):** uptrend structure — price makes progressively higher swing points.
- **Lower High (LH) / Lower Low (LL):** downtrend structure.
- **Break of Structure (BOS):** price breaks the most recent swing high (in an uptrend) or swing low (in a downtrend) in the direction of the existing trend — confirms trend continuation.
- **Change of Character (CHoCH):** the first break of structure *against* the prevailing trend — the earliest signal that a reversal may be forming. A CHoCH does not confirm a new trend by itself; it flags that the old trend's control has weakened.
- Practical rule: treat CHoCH as "pay attention," and a confirmed BOS in the new direction as "trend has likely shifted."

## 2. Liquidity (السيولة)
- **Buy-side liquidity (BSL):** resting sell-stop and breakout-buy orders sit just above obvious swing highs / equal highs.
- **Sell-side liquidity (SSL):** resting buy-stop and breakout-sell orders sit just below obvious swing lows / equal lows.
- **Liquidity sweep (stop hunt):** price pushes briefly beyond a high/low to trigger those resting orders, then reverses sharply. This is the mechanism institutions use to fill large orders without moving price against themselves before the real move.
- **Equal highs/lows (EQH/EQL):** two or more swing points at nearly the same level — a strong magnet for liquidity sweeps because retail traders cluster stops there.
- Practical rule: a sweep of an obvious high/low followed by a fast rejection candle back inside the range is a higher-probability reversal signal than a sweep with no rejection.

## 3. Order Blocks (كتل الأوامر)
- **Definition:** the last opposing candle (or small cluster of candles) before a strong, impulsive move away from that area. A bearish order block precedes a strong drop; a bullish order block precedes a strong rally.
- **Why it matters:** the theory is that large institutional orders were placed in that zone, and price tends to return to "mitigate" (partially fill) those orders before continuing.
- **Mitigation:** when price returns to an order block and reacts (reverses) from it, the block is considered "respected." If price closes fully through it, the block is considered "broken" or invalidated.
- **Refinement:** the tightest, most reactive entry is usually the last down-close (or up-close) candle's body/wick boundary within the block, not the whole block's full range.

## 4. Fair Value Gap (فجوة القيمة العادلة / FVG)
- **Definition:** a three-candle imbalance where the wick of candle 1 and the wick of candle 3 do not overlap — leaving a visible gap in candle 2's range. Represents a fast, one-sided move where price traded through with little two-way auction.
- **Behavior:** price frequently returns to "fill" or partially fill this gap before continuing in the original direction — used as a lower-risk re-entry zone rather than chasing the initial move.
- **Note:** not every FVG gets filled quickly; some remain open for long periods, especially in very strong trends.

## 5. Premium & Discount (المناطق المكلفة والرخيصة)
- Divide the most recent significant swing (from a swing low to a swing high, or vice versa) into an equilibrium at the 50% level.
- **Discount zone:** below 50% of the range — a more favorable area to look for buys.
- **Premium zone:** above 50% of the range — a more favorable area to look for sells.
- Combining this with order blocks and liquidity sweeps: the highest-quality setups typically occur when a sweep + order block + FVG all line up inside a discount zone (for buys) or premium zone (for sells).

## 6. Inducement (الإغراء)
- A minor liquidity pool (small swing high/low) placed *before* the real order block or major liquidity zone, designed (in SMC theory) to trap early entries before the actual move.
- Practical use: wait for the inducement level to be swept first, rather than entering at the first sign of reaction.

## 7. Putting It Together — A Simple SMC Read
1. Identify the higher-timeframe trend (structure of HH/HL or LH/LL).
2. Mark the last untouched order block and any nearby FVG in the direction of that trend.
3. Wait for a liquidity sweep of a nearby high/low (ideally inside a discount zone for longs, premium zone for shorts).
4. Look for a CHoCH/BOS on a lower timeframe confirming the reaction.
5. Enter with a stop beyond the sweep's extreme; target the next liquidity pool or opposing order block.

## Important Caveat
SMC/ICT terminology describes a way of *reading* price action after the fact; it is not a guaranteed predictive system, and no combination of these concepts produces certainty about future price movement. Treat every setup here as a framework for organizing observations, not a promise of profit. This material is educational only and is not financial advice.
