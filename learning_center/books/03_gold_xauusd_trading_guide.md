# Gold (XAUUSD) — Trading Reference Guide

## ملخص سريع (عربي)
الذهب سلعة ملاذ آمن وتتحرك أسعاره بتأثر قوي بالدولار الأمريكي (علاقة عكسية غالبًا)، والعوائد الحقيقية على السندات الأمريكية، وحالة المخاطر العامة في الأسواق (Risk-on/Risk-off). يتميز الذهب بتقلب أعلى من أزواج الفوركس الرئيسية خصوصًا خلال بيانات أمريكية مهمة (NFP، CPI، قرارات الفيدرالي)، وتختلف سلوكياته باختلاف الجلسات (آسيوية هادئة غالبًا، لندن تفتح الحركة، نيويورك الأقوى خصوصًا عند التداخل مع لندن).

## 1. What Drives Gold Prices
- **US Dollar (DXY) correlation:** gold is priced in USD, so a stronger dollar makes gold more expensive in other currencies (tends to pressure gold lower), and a weaker dollar tends to support gold — this inverse relationship is usually strong but not perfectly reliable minute-to-minute.
- **Real yields:** gold pays no interest, so it competes with interest-bearing assets like US Treasury bonds. When real (inflation-adjusted) yields rise, holding gold becomes relatively less attractive; when real yields fall, gold tends to become more attractive.
- **Safe-haven demand:** during geopolitical stress, banking-sector concerns, or sharp equity sell-offs, gold often attracts inflows as a perceived store of value, sometimes decoupling temporarily from its usual dollar/yield relationship.
- **Central bank demand:** official-sector gold buying (central banks adding to reserves) is a slower-moving but structurally important demand source.
- **Inflation expectations:** gold is often discussed as an inflation hedge, though the empirical relationship is more consistent over long horizons than on a day-to-day basis.

## 2. Session Behavior
- **Asian session:** typically the quietest range for gold; often used to define a tighter reference range that London/NY may later break.
- **London session (open):** frequently introduces the first strong directional push of the day and can set the tone others react to.
- **New York session:** carries the heaviest US data releases and typically the highest volume; the London/New York overlap window is generally the most liquid period of the day.
- **Practical implication:** ranges built in Asia are commonly swept during London or New York — treat Asian-session highs/lows as candidate liquidity levels rather than durable support/resistance.

## 3. High-Impact News Events for Gold
- **US Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP):** monthly US employment report; frequently produces sharp, fast moves in gold within the first minutes of release.
- **US CPI (inflation data):** directly affects real-yield expectations and Fed policy expectations, a common driver of large gold swings.
- **FOMC meetings/statements:** interest-rate decisions and forward guidance from the Federal Reserve are among the most reliable volatility triggers for gold.
- **Geopolitical headlines:** unscheduled and can cause abrupt gap-like moves; risk management around open conflict/crisis headlines should assume wider-than-normal ranges.
- Practical rule: reducing position size (or standing aside) heading into scheduled high-impact releases is a common risk-control practice, since spreads widen and slippage risk increases sharply in the seconds around release.

## 4. Volatility Characteristics
- Gold's typical daily range is generally wider (in percentage terms) than major FX pairs like EUR/USD, meaning position sizing needs to account for a larger average stop distance.
- ATR (Average True Range) is commonly used to normalize stop and target distances to current volatility rather than fixed pip counts, since a "good" stop distance in a quiet week can be far too tight in a high-volatility week.
- Volatility itself clusters — quiet periods tend to be followed by more quiet periods, and volatile periods tend to cluster together, until a regime-changing catalyst (data surprise, geopolitical shock) arrives.

## 5. Applying SMC / Price Action to Gold Specifically
- Because gold is heavily news-driven, liquidity sweeps around scheduled releases are common and often more violent than in calmer FX pairs — waiting for the immediate post-release volatility to settle before evaluating an SMC setup can reduce whipsaw risk.
- Round numbers (e.g., prices ending in 00 or 50) often act as informal psychological support/resistance in gold specifically, adding a layer of confluence to structural levels.
- The DXY chart is a useful secondary confirmation: a gold breakout that is not accompanied by a corresponding move (in the opposite direction) in DXY is somewhat less confirmed than one that lines up with a clear dollar move.

## Important Caveat
Gold is influenced by a wide, overlapping set of macro factors that can shift in relative importance over time (e.g., safe-haven flows can temporarily overwhelm the usual dollar/yield relationship). No fixed rule set captures all of this reliably, and past correlations can break down. This material is educational only and is not financial advice; it does not predict future gold price movements.
